Broker-dealer registration questions are not purely fee-triggered | P0 (procedural — a launch-blocker-in-spirit, not a code defect) | Owner + Counsel | Do not add a per-close success fee or any transaction-based investor compensation until a securities attorney reviews the introduction/matching mechanism itself | Open — flagged for `COUNSEL/OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED`, carried to Section 17 |
| R-03 | 01 | No third-party error-monitoring/log-aggregation (Sentry-style) exists server-side; a production incident may go undetected until a user reports it | P1 | Engineering (Section 14) | Wire structured error monitoring + alerting before scaling past the current low-traffic stage | Open — carried to Section 14 |
